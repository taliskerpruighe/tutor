The affiant, Lauren Rae Garth, having been duly sworn, states as follows:
I, Lauren Rae Garth, an Australian citizen, am the applicant and beneficiary in this case.  A copy of my birth certificate is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 6.  An original issue of Exhibit 6 was included with the petition submitted in this case.  
I am submitting this affidavit in support of Petitioner Brent S. Jacobs’ Combined Motion for Reopening and Reconsideration. 

On May 7, 2015, I entered the United States pursuant to a valid K-1 non-immigrant visa previously requested by my then fiancé, Brent Steven Jacobs.  Ever since then, I have resided in Mr. Jacobs’ physical and mailing address: 212 Olde Pulley Lane, Apartment L, Menasha, WI 54952.

On June 12, 2015, Mr. Jacobs and I married in Oshkosh, Wisconsin.  A copy of our marriage certificate is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 2.  An original issue of Exhibit 2 was included with the petition submitted in this case. 

On July 29, 2016, Mr. Jacobs filed a petition to adjust my status to that of a permanent resident of the United States.  On August 8, 2016, Mr. Jacobs and I received an I-797C Notice of Action indicating that the Service had received and begun processing the petition.  A copy of that receipt is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 3.  This notice was delivered by the United States Postal Service (“USPS”) to Mr. Jacobs’ physical and mailing address without problem or delay.

On August 8, 2016, Mr. Jacobs and I received an I-797C Notice of Action indicating that the Service had scheduled my biometrics appointment for August 25, 2015.  A copy of that receipt is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 4.  This notice was delivered by USPS to Mr. Jacobs’ physical and mailing address without problem or delay.  I attended and successfully completed her biometrics appointment.

Between August 25, 2015 and February of 2016, I received no further communications regarding this case.  During this period, USPS did not deliver any notices or other communications regarding this case to Mr. Jacobs’ physical and mailing address.  During this period, USPS also did not notify me of any pending or missed deliveries addressed to me or to Mr. Jacobs’ physical and mailing address.  

In March of 2016, Mr. Jacobs and I contacted our attorney in this case, Marcel Oliveira, and requested that he contact the Service on our behalf to inquire about the status of our case.  Following phone and written inquiries to the Service, Mr. Oliveira relayed that the Service had record that a notice in the case had been returned to the Service because USPS could not deliver it.  

At that point, I immediately submitted a written inquiry to the Service.  The written inquiry explained that neither Mr. Jacobs nor I had received a notice, or any information regarding USPS’ failed delivery of the notice.  The written inquiry’s identification number is SR11081600493MSC.  A copy of that written inquiry is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 7.  

In response to my written inquiry, the Service provided email communication stating that a response would be processed by May 9, 2016.  A copy of that response is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 8.  

However, on May 3, 2016, I received a denial letter in this case from the Service.  A copy of that letter is enclosed to this affidavit as Exhibit 9.  The letter stated that the petition Mr. Jacobs had filed on my behalf had been denied for failure to appear in the interview whose date was provided in the notice I did not receive.  

Other than the notices enclosed as Exhibits 3 and 4, the reply enclosed as Exhibit 8, and the denial letter enclosed as Exhibit 9, I have received no other communications from the Service in this case, via mail or otherwise.  

I failed to appear for the interview solely because I had no notice of the interview, and no notice of the fact that the Service’s notice scheduling the interview had failed to reach me.

If called to testify in any proceeding in this case, I would testify as stated in this affidavit.
