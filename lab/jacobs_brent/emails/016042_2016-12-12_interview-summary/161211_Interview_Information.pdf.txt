      This letter provides an overview of the interview process for a marriage-based
adjustment of status application.

The Day of the Interview

       Your interview will likely take place in the Milwaukee Field Office in downtown
Milwaukee. USCIS schedules interviews in “time blocks”: more than one couple is
scheduled for any given time slot during the day and, within that time frame, couples are
seen on a first-come, first-serve basis. Therefore, the earlier you arrive on the day of your
interview, the earlier your interview will take place within your time slot. Your time slot
will be indicated in your notice of interview.

       You should plan to arrive in Milwaukee several hours ahead of your interview.
While you can reschedule interviews in cases of emergencies, such as a transportation
problem on the way to Milwaukee, that can severely delay your case. USCIS does not
take kindly to interview rescheduling – particularly not in the last minute – so it should
be left as a last resort. If your interview is early in the morning, you should arrive in
Milwaukee the night before.

       Both of you will need to attend the interview. In easier cases, meaning cases where

there is significant evidence of an existing relationship, USCIS officers will interview the

      The purpose of interviews in marriage-based adjustment of status cases is to
confirm that the relationship is real, and not just a way to obtain a green card. Therefore,
most of the questions USCIS officers ask have to do with determining how well you two
know each other, and how well you two can describe your lives together since you met.

       Below are several sample interview questions that you two could get in the
adjustment of status interview. Please note that these questions are only estimates of what
could be asked: USCIS interviewers can ask virtually anything they want upon reviewing
your file, and do not have to cover any particular topic.
