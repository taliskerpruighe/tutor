Just so you know, since this is a reopening, I would not be surprised it if took another couple of weeks for the notice to arrive. The case was closed and archived; so USCIS has to dig it back up, put it back on some officer’s desk, and reissue the notice.

The people who adjudicate reopening are not the same as the people who adjudicate the actual case — so they may have some catching up to do. So, in case it does not arrive this week, I would not be too concerned. 





Do we know whether the case we keep the same case numbers? That should be the case but, technically, USCIS has the right to play around with that as they see fit. If your receipts are keeping the same case numbers, you can track them online. The I-130 has

one case number, and the I-485 has another — so I would make sure to check them both.
