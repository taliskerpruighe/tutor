                    INTERNATIONAL TRADE AGREEMENT

                                      INTRODUCTION

This International Trade Agreement (this "Agreement"), dated as of December 7, 2025, is
entered into by and between Caren Braun LLC, a Minnesota limited liability company (the
"Seller"), and Dominique Corazon Braun-Ibardolaza (the "Buyer").

                                         RECITALS

A. The Seller is a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Minnesota,
   United States of America, with its principal place of business at 917 E. 10th Street, Duluth,
   MN 55805.

B. The Buyer is an individual residing in the Republic of the Philippines, with their principal
   address at Blk 8 Lot 13 Camella Homes brgy Joyao-Joyao Numancia Aklan, Philippines.

C. The Seller is engaged in the business of sourcing, purchasing, and exporting consumer goods
   from the United States to international markets.

D. The Buyer desires to purchase consumer goods from the Seller for resale or distribution in
   the Philippines, and the Seller desires to sell such goods to the Buyer, on the terms and
   conditions set forth in this Agreement.

E. The Parties intend this Agreement to establish a continuous and ongoing trading relationship
   involving numerous transactions over time.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein,
and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby
acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

                                       DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms in this Agreement shall have the following meanings:

"Agreement" means this International Trade Agreement, including all schedules, exhibits, and
amendments hereto.

"Buyer" means Dominique Corazon Braun-Ibardolaza, the party identified in the Introduction as
purchasing Goods from the Seller.
"Goods" means the consumer products, merchandise, and other items of trade that the Seller
agrees to sell and the Buyer agrees to purchase under this Agreement, as specified in each
Purchase Order.

"Incoterms" means the International Commercial Terms published by the International
Chamber of Commerce, as amended from time to time. Unless otherwise specified in a Purchase
Order, all shipments under this Agreement shall be made FOB (Free on Board) point of shipment
in the United States.

"Purchase Order" means a written order submitted by the Buyer to the Seller specifying the
Goods to be purchased, quantities, prices, delivery instructions, and other relevant terms.

"Seller" means Caren Braun LLC, a Minnesota limited liability company.

                     ARTICLE 1. SALE AND PURCHASE OF GOODS

1.1.    Agreement to Sell and Purchase. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement,
    the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Goods specified in Purchase
    Orders submitted by the Buyer and accepted by the Seller during the Term of this
    Agreement.

1.2.    Nature of Trade. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the transactions contemplated
    by this Agreement constitute international trade between the United States of America and
    the Republic of the Philippines. Title to the Goods shall pass from the Seller in the United
    States to the Buyer in the Philippines upon delivery in accordance with the applicable
    Incoterms.

1.3.    Continuous Trading Relationship. The Parties intend for this Agreement to establish a
    continuous and ongoing trading relationship. The Buyer agrees to submit Purchase Orders on
    a regular and recurring basis throughout the Term, with the expectation of at least two (2)
    Purchase Orders per month.

1.4.    Categories of Goods. The Goods to be traded under this Agreement may include, but are
    not limited to:

    a. Consumer electronics and accessories;

    b. Health and beauty products;
    c. Household goods and appliances;

    d. Clothing and apparel;

    e. Food products and supplements (subject to applicable import regulations);

    f. Such other consumer goods as the Parties may agree upon from time to time.

                             ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE ORDERS

2.1.    Submission of Purchase Orders. The Buyer shall submit Purchase Orders to the Seller in
    writing (including by electronic mail) specifying:

    a. A description of the Goods requested;

    b. The quantity of each item;

    c. The requested Delivery Date;

    d. The delivery address in the Philippines;

    e. Any special shipping or handling instructions.

2.2.   Acceptance of Purchase Orders. The Seller shall confirm acceptance of each Purchase
    Order within five (5) business days of receipt. The Seller may reject any Purchase Order, or
    any portion thereof, in its reasonable discretion. A Purchase Order shall become binding
    upon the Parties only upon the Seller's written acceptance.

                           ARTICLE 3. PRICES AND PAYMENT

3.1.    Prices. The price for each item of Goods shall be as specified in the applicable Purchase
    Order. All prices are stated in United States Dollars (USD) unless otherwise agreed in
    writing.

3.2.    Payment Terms. Unless otherwise specified in a Purchase Order, the Buyer shall pay the
    full purchase price for each order according to the following terms: one hundred percent
    (100%) of the purchase price prior to shipment.
3.3.    Method of Payment. All payments shall be made by wire transfer to the Seller's
    designated bank account in the United States, or by such other method as the Parties may
    agree in writing. The Buyer shall bear all bank fees and transfer charges.

                          ARTICLE 4. DELIVERY AND SHIPPING

4.1.    Delivery Terms. Unless otherwise specified in a Purchase Order, all Goods shall be
    delivered FOB (Free on Board) point of shipment in the United States, in accordance with
    Incoterms 2020. Risk of loss shall pass to the Buyer upon delivery of the Goods to the
    carrier.

4.2.    Shipping. The Seller shall arrange for shipment of the Goods to the Buyer's designated
    address in the Philippines. Shipment may be made via balikbayan box, international courier,
    freight forwarder, or such other method as the Parties may agree. The Buyer shall bear all
    shipping, freight, insurance, customs duties, and other costs associated with the international
    transport of the Goods.

4.3.   Documentation. The Seller shall provide the Buyer with all documentation reasonably
    necessary for the importation of the Goods into the Philippines, including commercial
    invoices, packing lists, and certificates of origin where applicable.

                                 ARTICLE 5. WARRANTIES

5.1.   Seller's Warranties. The Seller warrants that:

    a. The Seller has good title to the Goods and the right to sell them;

    b. The Goods shall conform to the descriptions and specifications set forth in the applicable
       Purchase Order;

    c. The Goods shall be free from material defects in workmanship at the time of delivery.

            ARTICLE 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND COMPLIANCE

6.1.    Compliance with Laws. Each Party shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations,
    and governmental requirements in connection with the performance of this Agreement,
    including without limitation all import and export laws and regulations of the United States
    and the Philippines.
6.2.    Import Permits. The Buyer shall be solely responsible for obtaining any import permits,
    licenses, or approvals required for the importation of the Goods into the Philippines.

                                      ARTICLE 8. TERM

8.1.   Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall
    continue for an initial term of two (2) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with
    Section 9.

8.2.    Renewal. Upon expiration of the initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew
    for successive periods of one (1) year each, unless either Party provides written notice of
    non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

                                ARTICLE 9. TERMINATION

9.1.   Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time
    upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

9.2.    Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon
    written notice if the other Party:

    a. Breaches any material provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within
       thirty (30) days after receiving written notice thereof;

    b. Becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or makes an assignment for the benefit of
       creditors.

                           ARTICLE 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.2. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
    the laws of the State of Minnesota, United States of America, without regard to its conflict of
    laws principles.

                           ARTICLE 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
    with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and
    negotiations, whether written or oral.
12.2. Amendments. This Agreement may not be amended or modified except by a written
    instrument signed by both Parties.

12.6. Notices. All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given
    when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) days after being
    sent by registered or certified mail to the addresses set forth below or to such other address as
    either Party may designate in writing.
