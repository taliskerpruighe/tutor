> Yes, I still do LLC's and yes, one would help your situation. I am not
> sure whether your work restrictions apply only to W-2 salaried work, or
> both W-2 and 1099 independent contractor work. But either way, the LLC will
> help. If your restrictions are only for W-2 work, you can work for the LLC
> or through the LLC as a 1099. If your restrictions also extend to 1099
> work, you can still be an owner of the LLC and take the equivalent of
> dividends--and then "work" as much as you want without raising any flags.
> And in either of those scenarios, the LLC can deduct any costs that are
> business-related, which does save you a bunch of cash on taxes.
>
