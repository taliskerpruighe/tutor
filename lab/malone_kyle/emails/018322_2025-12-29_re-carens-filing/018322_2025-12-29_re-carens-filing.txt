> All good questions. This EIN/responsible party thing is a an unnecessary complication on the IRS' part.
> 
> Basically, to get an EIN, a company needs to provide what they call a "responsible party." A responsible party is NOT responsible for tax liabilities. The responsible party is just the contact person for taxes, like a "tax partner" in a partnership.
> 
> The catch is the SSN. To apply online for an EIN and get one on the spot, you have to provide an SSN for the responsible party/contact person. If the contact person does not have one--for example, sometimes the person is foreign, other times the "person" is actually a company--you have to send the form in via fax (of all things) and wait several weeks.
> 
> So to answer your question directly, there is no worry about the IRS asking you questions about the LLC, your involvement with it, or the visa. At least I have never heard of that happening. Since it does not make you an owner or liable in any way, there is no "mixing."
>
