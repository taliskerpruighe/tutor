## 4. Depreciation policy and application

Fixed assets are recorded at cost and depreciated on a straight-line basis, no salvage value assumed, beginning the month following the date each asset is placed in service. By asset class: Fixed Assets - Equipment (account 1500), including fabrication machinery, is depreciated over a 7-year useful life (approximately 14.29% per year); Fixed Assets - Vehicles (account 1510) is depreciated over a 5-year useful life (20% per year).

- Existing fabrication equipment (cost $210,438.87, in service before the opening date): $2,505.22/month, all 12 months of 2025 = $30,062.64.
- Existing work vehicle (cost $42,000.00): $700.00/month, all 12 months = $8,400.00.
- Press brake (cost $151,200.00, placed in service 2025-04-20, depreciation begins the following month per policy): $1,800.00/month, May-Dec 2025 (8 months) = $14,400.00.
- Total 2025 depreciation expense: $52,862.64. Opening accumulated depreciation $29,326.10 + 2025 depreciation $52,862.64 = closing accumulated depreciation $82,188.74, agreeing with the balance sheet above.

## 5. Opening AR/AP settlement (Rule Two)

The opening letter's accounts receivable and accounts payable are prior-period balances. Their in-period cash settlement is recorded as a pure balance-sheet movement (debit/credit Cash against the AR or AP balance carried over from the opening entry) and never touches Sales Revenue or an expense account, so it does not distort 2025's P&L:
