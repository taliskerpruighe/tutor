   Jesus:

Apologies, I have been behind on email. 

Generally, you should only reschedule interview only if you absolutely must. To ask for a new interview date, you must have a "good cause.” 

USCIS does not set out a rule for good cause. Something like a family or medical emergency always qualifies as good cause. A business trip may qualify as good cause—but there is no guarantee. If the trip cannot be changed and, most importantly, cannot be refunded, then you may be able to reschedule. But again, no guarantees.

If you want to try it, I can help you with a formal, written request based on the USCIS rules. It would be a decent amount of work, so I would have to add a separate charge for it; but I would cap it at one hour ($500).

Do you want to give it a try? Or is the trip something you can change?
