## THE DELEGATED-CORRESPONDENCE SHAPE — a new intake pattern
The applicant is not the correspondent. Kyle Malone (US citizen, Duluth MN) is
the coordinator for three separate family matters. The applicant, in the
Philippines, is frequently unreachable and unresponsive.

Kyle, 2025-01-29, quotable:
"Talked to Caren just now…She has no questionnaire and her last email from you
was the 27th of December. Please copy me on emails and I'll make sure she has
them and works them."

The firm's own escalation formula: "Given the urgency, I am copying Kyle so
that we do not miss the deadline."

**This is a distinct synthetic-client shape worth building: a matter where a
relative does all the corresponding and the applicant barely appears.**

  embassy deadlines.

- Applicant travelling during the critical window.
