# izaguirre_jesus — intake chronology (runner report)

"AT" in `Intake/Izaguirre, Jesus AT/` = AIRTABLE. Confirmed by the Airtable
export and grid-view tracker in the same folder.

## THE AIRTABLE QUESTIONNAIRE — full field list, verbatim
1. Please upload a picture or scan of your current green card, front and back.
2. Please upload a picture or scan of the bio (picture) page of your latest
   passport.
3. Please upload a picture or scan of your latest driver's license or state ID,
   front and back.
4. Please upload your tax return/extension for the last three fiscal years:
   (1) 2020, (2) 2019, and (3) 2018. The 1040 only is fine: you do not need
   the entire return.
5. If you would like to legally change your name when you get your citizenship,
   enter it here.
6. For each residential address you have had *since you received your green
   card,* please enter: (1) the full address, (2) the approximate month/year of
   move-in, and (3) the approximate month/year of move out.
7. For each job/position you have had *since you received your green card,*
   please enter: (1) the job title, (2) the employer's name, (3) the employer's
   address, (4) the approximate month/year the job stated [sic], and (5) the
   approximate month/year the job ended.
8. For each trip you have taken outside the United States *since you received
   your green card,* please enter: (1) the countries visited, (2) the
   approximate day, month and year in which you departed the United States,
   and (3) the approximate day, month and year in which you returned to the
   United States.
9. If you have gotten married since you obtained your green card, please enter:
   (1) your spouse's full name, (2) the date and city of marriage, (3) your
   spouse's country of birth, (4) your spouse's current employer, and (5) your
   spouse's current address.
10. If you have had any children since you obtained your green card, for each
    child, please enter: (1) full name, (2) country of birth, and (3) current
    address.

Note the typo "the job stated" for "started" — present in the firm's own form.
Note the anchor phrase, repeated: "since you received your green card".
This 2021 version asks for a driver's licence and THREE years of tax returns;
the 2024 version asks for a resume and ONE year. It also asks about a name
change, which the later form drops, and omits the "other citizenships"
question the later form adds.

## Messiness

  left BLANK on first submission. Trips arrived months later, separately.
- Asked for three years of tax returns; supplied 2018, 2019 and an extension.
  No 2020.
- Documents arrive as PNG photos; receipts as JPGs on a wooden surface.
- Client did not have his prior passport at interview, triggering an RFE.
