## THE CLEANEST INPUT/OUTPUT SPLIT IN THE CORPUS
Unlike jacobs_brent and zhu_vivian, this matter partitions explicitly:
`Intake/` = client material + drafts + admin. `Deliverables/` = the filed
packet. `Engagement/` = the contract. Runner rates all four conventions HIGH.

Naming differs by side: Intake drafts use messy prefixes
(`Jesus Izaguirre 3_ N-400.pdf`, `7c_ Exhibit A.pdf`), Deliverables use clean
ones (`3. N-400.pdf`, `7a. Exhibit A.pdf`). Working vs filed.
