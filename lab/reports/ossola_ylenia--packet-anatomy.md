```
January 30, 2023

VIA FEDERAL EXPRESS
[same Chicago lockbox block]

	Re:	Case Number: IOE9758622666
		Applicant: Ylenia Ossola

```
A SUPPLEMENTAL filing, ten months later, sending in the unconditional green
card once the I-751 was approved. RE: block switches from form name to CASE
NUMBER. No signature block. Note "receival" — the firm's own malapropism.

**This is a document type no other matter has: a mid-flight supplement to a
pending application.** Worth reproducing in the synthetic set.
