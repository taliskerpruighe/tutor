# zhu_vivian — intake chronology (runner report)

## THE INTAKE QUESTIONNAIRE — full field list
Source: `N-400 Naturalization/Zhu, Vivian/Others/Questionnaire - Vivian Zhu.docx`
Offered both as a docx and as an online Jotform.

Intro text, verbatim:
"Below please find your questionnaire for naturalization application. Some
questions will ask for supporting documents. To make things easier, we have
created an online form for you, if you'd prefer to use it. Please note: if you
do not have all answers, or all documents, that is not a problem. Just provide
what you have: some answers are helpful, but not strictly required."

Fields:
1. Current physical address (street, city, state/province, postal code, country)
2. Date of residence: From (mm/dd/yyyy)
3. Any other citizenships? If yes, list.
4. Every country visited in the past 5 years, with arrival and departure dates
   (excluding the United States).
5. Marital status (Single / Married / Divorced / Widowed / Separated /
   Marriage Annulled)
6. For current or prior spouse: legal name, date of birth, present physical
   address, present employer, date marriage started (and ended, if applicable).
Documents requested:
7. Your latest resume, even if outdated
8. Copy of the 1040 (first two pages) of the last federal tax return filed
9. Picture or scan of the bio page of your latest passport
10. Picture or scan of your latest green card (front and back ideally)

2. A year rendered "202" instead of "2023"
3. Travel dates in the questionnaire not carried into the form
4. A Singapore day trip included when instructions said exclude day trips
5. Address date range needed a manual override
6. Arrest/offence description incomplete

## Client voice
Brief, professional, complete sentences, signs off "Thanks, Viv". Answers
questions on the form tersely and sometimes with a query of her own ("DK. must
i provide?", "what are the benefits if check yes?"). Turns impatient by late
December and frankly profane by June ("This is a bitch gov. And I'm pressured
by company...").

## Pattern worth copying
Client returns the structured form fast, but the returned data is thin and
partly wrong. Corrections then come back as EMAIL PROSE WITH SCREENSHOTS
rather than a corrected form. Documents arrive as phone photos and a locked
PDF. The firm discovers the shortfalls only while drafting.
