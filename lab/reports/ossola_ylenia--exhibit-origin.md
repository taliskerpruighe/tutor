## The marriage-evidence cluster
Four exhibits exist solely to prove the marriage is genuine:
- 8. spouse's passport -> he is a US citizen
- 10. joint deed -> they own a home together
- 11. joint auto policy -> shared household and financial obligation
- 12. child's passport -> a child born to the marriage

None is a standard naturalization exhibit. All four are I-751-style bona fide
marriage evidence carried forward into an N-400. The firm over-documents.

## Updated canonical checklist — five packets
ALWAYS: index/TOC · cover letter · N-400 · applicant passport bio page ·
green card copy.
SITUATIONAL, each triggered by a form answer:
- card payment -> G-1450
- e-notification requested -> G-1145
- spousal basis -> USC spouse's passport, and often joint deed / policy / tax
  return / child's passport
- conditional resident still awaiting the I-751 -> the I-751 receipt (I-797C)
- extensive travel -> travel addendum (zhu) or .xlsx addendum (izaguirre)
- arrest history -> court records (zhu)
- removal proceedings -> written explanation + prior-case exhibit run
  (izaguirre)
- a later development on a live case -> a supplemental cover letter (ossola)
