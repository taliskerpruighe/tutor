## VERDICT — a THIRD variant, and the earliest
Shares with jacobs: flat numeric prefixes, FedEx, no merged PDF.
Shares with zhu: the SYMPLE / "By: Marcel S. Oliveira / Petition Preparer"
signature block, and the RE: block carrying applicant + COB/CON.
Unique to izaguirre: "INDEX OF DOCUMENTS" wording, mixed .docx/.xlsx/.pdf
delivery, an Exhibit A-G lettering scheme layered under numeric prefixes, the
xlsx travel addendum, the "MSO / Encls." block, Lewisville TX lockbox, and a
numbering slip (two 3s, Written Explanation numbered 7 alongside 7a-7g).
