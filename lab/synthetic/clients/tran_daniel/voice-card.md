# T1 — `tran_daniel` — VOICE CARD

**Register label:** `verbose-solution-oriented-delegate`
**Corpus lineage:** the malone register — described from `lab/reports/`, never
quoted. Every line below is invented for this file.
**Speakers:** two, unevenly. Daniel writes 90% of the words; Ha writes three or
four short messages in the whole thread.

## Daniel — texture

Native-speaker American English, fluent, organised, and far too long. He does
not just answer the question: he proposes a **process** for answering it. He
numbers his paragraphs. He anticipates the next three questions and answers
those too, sometimes wrongly. Sentences run **20 to 35 words**. He restates the
firm's question back before answering it, which doubles the length of every
message.

He is helpful in a way that occasionally creates work — he will summarise a
document rather than send it, then send it two messages later.

## Daniel — punctuation habits

Numbered lists (`1.`, `2.`, `3.`) inside otherwise ordinary prose. Bold-by-caps
for emphasis on a single word. Em dashes. He closes messages with a
forward-looking sentence: "Let me know what you need next and I'll get it
turned around tonight."

## Daniel — greeting and sign-off

Greeting: `Hi —` or `Following up on this:`. Sign-off: `Thanks, Daniel`.
Occasional "Sent from my phone, apologies for typos" that is followed by no
typos.

## Daniel — emotional temperature

Confident, mildly proprietorial about the matter, entirely well-meaning. He
treats the filing as a project he is managing. When something goes wrong — the
e-signature link — he does not complain; he routes around it and tells you what
he did.

## Daniel — being asked for something twice

He apologises briefly, then over-corrects: sends the thing, plus the two
adjacent things, plus an explanation of his filing system.

## Ha — texture

Short, careful, correct. Six to fifteen words. She writes only to fix a fact
Daniel has slightly wrong, and she is always right. Slight formality: `Thank
you.` as a complete message. No comma splices — her English is textbook-learnt
and she is careful with it.

Any confusion, from either of them, about who the applicant is — they both know;
the folder name is the only thing that is misleading.
