# T2 — `stavros_daphne` — VOICE CARD

**Register label:** `warm-over-deliverer-self-doubting`
**Corpus lineage:** the ossola register — described from `lab/reports/`, never
quoted. Every line below is invented for this file.
**Speaker:** Daphne, the daughter. Her father does not write; he is quoted
("my father says…", "I asked him again and he says…").

## Texture

Native-speaker American English, warm, careful, and permanently unsure that
she has done enough. Sentences run **18 to 30 words** and often end in a
hedge — "I think", "if that's useful", "unless you'd rather have the original".
She writes in the second person a lot ("you'll probably want…"), because she is
imagining the reader's needs and trying to get ahead of them.

She sends **more than was asked for, every time**, and apologises for it in the
same breath.

## Punctuation habits

Parentheses for hedges. Question marks on statements ("It's the 2024 one?").
Ellipses when she trails off into doubt. Occasional bold or ALL-CAPS on a
single word she is worried you will miss. Her spreadsheet is immaculate; her
prose is not.

## Greeting and sign-off

Greeting: `Hi,` then, in the first message of the thread, a full and explicit
identification: who she is, who her father is, and that she is writing for him.
Sign-off: `Thank you so much, Daphne` — and, in the early messages, `Daphne
Stavros (Konstantinos's daughter)`.

## Emotional temperature

High-warmth, low-confidence. She is anxious about the 2015 immigration history
in a way that shows as *volume* — she explains it three times, each time
slightly differently, each time accurately. She is protective of her father and
mildly guilty that the 2015 denial happened partly because mail went to an old
address.

## Being asked for something twice

Immediate apology, the thing, and then two more things she now suspects might
also be needed. She never says "I already sent that" even when she has.

## Do not write

Brusqueness. Any message in which she is confident. Any suggestion that she is
the applicant — the folder name is the only thing that suggests that, and it is
the mess, not her.
