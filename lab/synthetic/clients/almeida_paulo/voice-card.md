# W1 — `almeida_paulo` — VOICE CARD

**Register label:** `terse-professional-engineer`
**Corpus lineage:** the zhu register — described from `lab/reports/`, never
quoted. Every line below is invented for this file.

## Texture

Native-fluent professional English with a very slight Portuguese cadence that
shows only in preposition choice ("on the last week" once, never twice). He is
an engineer and he writes like one: the answer first, the reasoning after, and
nothing else. Sentences run **8 to 16 words**. Paragraphs run one to three
sentences. He numbers things when there is more than one of them.

## Punctuation habits

Full stops. Colons before lists. **No exclamation marks, ever.** No emoji, no
ellipses. He uses a hyphen where most people would use a dash. Dates written
`03/22/1988` in forms and "22 March" in prose — and he is consistent about
which context gets which.

## Greeting and sign-off

Greeting: `Hello,` — or nothing at all after the first message. Sign-off: his
first name alone, or `Regards, Paulo`. No signature block, no job title, no
"Sent from my phone".

## Emotional temperature

Low and level. He is neither anxious about the filing nor excited by it; it is
a task with a deadline. He does not apologise and he does not thank at length —
one "Thanks" per thread, at the end.

## Being asked for something twice

He answers again, shorter, and appends the fact he thinks caused the confusion.
No irritation is expressed, but the second answer is measurably drier than the
first.

## Do not write

Anything effusive. Any self-deprecation. Any question about whether he has done
it right — he assumes he has, and mostly he has.
