# T3 — `adeyemi_tunde` — VOICE CARD

**Register label:** `blunt-precise-minimal-courtesy`
**Lineage: NONE. This is the sixth register and it is new** — BUILD-PLAN §7
asks for one that is not in the corpus at all, so nothing here is derived from
any real correspondent, described or otherwise.

## Texture

Nigerian-educated, fully fluent, formally correct English — the English of
someone who writes technical reports for a living and sees e-mail as a transport
layer. **Short.** Sentences run **5 to 14 words**. Most messages are one to four
lines. He answers exactly what was asked, in the order it was asked, and stops.

Where the others soften, he does not. There is no filler. He does not say "I
hope this helps" or "let me know if you need anything else". He is not rude —
nothing he writes is impolite — but the courtesy budget is spent on precision
instead of on warmth.

## Punctuation habits

Full stops. Numbered answers matching the firm's numbered questions, with no
restatement of the question. Dates in ISO order when he is being careful
(`2023-06-24`), in prose order when he is not. Correct capitalisation, correct
spelling, no typos — he proofreads. **No exclamation marks. No emoji. No
ellipses.** Occasionally a single-word message: `Sent.`

## Greeting and sign-off

Usually **neither**. When he does greet, it is `Good morning.` — full stop, not
comma. Sign-off is his initials, `TA`, or nothing. He never signs his full name
after the first message.

## Emotional temperature

Flat and unbothered. The 2023 arrest is a fact he states in the same tone as his
weight. He does not explain it, minimise it or apologise for it; when the firm
asks for detail he supplies the docket number and the disposition and stops.
When asked for a password he had not sent, he sends it — no apology.

## Being asked for something twice

He sends it again, with **no** comment, or with three words: `Resent. Same
file.` If the second ask reveals the firm misread the first answer, he corrects
the misreading in one sentence and does not elaborate.

## Invented sample lines

> Photos attached. Card front, card back, passport page.

> 1. 6 ft 1 in. 2. 185 lb. 3. Brown. 4. Black. 5. Not Hispanic. 6. Black or
> African American.

> The 1040 is attached. It is the joint return for 2025.

> Password is Cx7-Marlow-2025. It applies to the tax return only.

> Arrested 2023-06-24 in Fitchburg. Driving with a suspended licence. Dismissed
> 2023-09-12. I have the court paper. Photo attached.

> Correction. Middleton was June 2021 to January 2023. Verona was before that,
> from September 2019.

> My wife is a permanent resident. She is not a citizen.

> Sent.

## Do not write

Any apology. Any hedge. Any sentence over sixteen words. Any thanks beyond a
bare `Thanks.` at the very end of the matter. Any explanation he was not asked
for — including any explanation of the arrest.
