# How to build one

*v0.1.0*

You are not going to write a `SKILL.md` by hand. There is a skill for
writing skills, and it does the file, the folder, the frontmatter and the
supporting files.

What it cannot do is decide what the skill should say. That part is yours,
and it is the only part that was ever yours — the same way a trainee can
type a letter but cannot know how your firm writes one.

## There is no wrong way to say it

This matters more than it sounds. Because Claude has the `custom-skills`
skill loaded, it adjusts to however much you give it.

Give it almost nothing — *"I want a skill for letters before action"* —
and it interviews you. What do you do first. What do you check. What have
you got in writing. It pulls the skill out of you a question at a time.

Give it a great deal — your house style, three precedent letters, the
protocol you follow and the four things you have been caught out on — and
it stops asking and starts writing.

Both work. You cannot brief it badly enough to break it, and there is no
form of words it is waiting for. A half-remembered sentence on a Tuesday
morning is a valid starting point.

So do not save this up for a clear afternoon. The clear afternoon is not
coming, and the skill does not need one.

## But there is an efficient way

The difference between a vague brief and a good one is not effort. It is
**what kind of thing you hand over.**

Most people, asked to explain a job, describe it. *"Always put the parties
in the caption. Use the short form after the first citation. Don't say
'clearly'."* That is a list of rules, and rules are the slowest way to
transfer a craft — you write them one at a time, you forget nine tenths of
them, and every one you forget is one the skill will never have.

The alternative is to show it. Hand over the work itself: what came in,
what went out, and the file as it looked in between. Everything you would
have said in a rule is in there already, along with the hundred rules you
would never have thought to say — the ones you stopped noticing you were
following somewhere around your third year.

That is leading by example, and it is the Boss's fourth Party Trick. It is
the next article, and it is the one thing in this part worth doing
deliberately.
