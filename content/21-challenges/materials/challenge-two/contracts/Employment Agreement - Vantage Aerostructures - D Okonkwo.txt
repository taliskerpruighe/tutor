EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (this Agreement) is entered into as of April
19, 2022, by and between Vantage Aerostructures Corp., a Connecticut
corporation with its principal offices in Stratford, Connecticut (the
Company), and Daniel Okonkwo, an individual residing in Connecticut
(Employee). The Company and Employee may be referred to individually
as a Party and together as the Parties.

RECITALS

The Company designs, develops, and manufactures structural components
and subassemblies for commercial and military aircraft, and has invested
substantial resources over many years in proprietary manufacturing
processes, tooling designs, and process engineering methods used in
that work. The Company is employing Employee as a Manufacturing Process
Engineer, a position in which Employee will have regular access to
the Company's proprietary manufacturing processes, tooling designs,
unreleased program information, and other information the Company
treats as confidential. The Company's manufacturing process engineering
group maintains detailed process specifications, tooling drawings,
yield data, and production ramp schedules for each aircraft program
the Company supports, and treats this information as among its most
sensitive because it reflects years of iterative engineering work and
is directly transferable to a competing manufacturing operation. In
consideration of Employee's employment, the base salary and benefits
Employee will receive under the Company's separate offer letter, and
Employee's resulting access to the Company's proprietary manufacturing
processes and confidential information described in this Agreement,
the Parties agree as follows.

POSITION AND DUTIES

Employee shall serve as a Manufacturing Process Engineer for the
Company, responsible for developing, documenting, and improving the
manufacturing processes and tooling used to produce the Company's
structural aerospace components and subassemblies. Employee
shall devote substantially all of Employee's working time and
best efforts to the performance of these duties, shall follow the
Company's engineering change control and documentation procedures,
and shall comply with the Company's policies concerning access to
and handling of proprietary manufacturing data, program schedules,
and customer specifications. Employee shall report to the Company's
Director of Manufacturing Engineering or such other person as the
Company designates from time to time.

DEFINITIONS

Competing Products means any structural aerospace component,
subassembly, or manufacturing process substantially similar to,
or competitive with, the products, subassemblies, or manufacturing
processes designed, developed, or manufactured by the Company as of
the date Employee's employment with the Company terminates.

Facility means any plant, factory, laboratory, or other physical
location, wherever situated within the United States, at which
design, engineering, tooling, or manufacturing work on Competing
Products is carried out, regardless of the corporate ownership,
size, or name of the entity that operates the location.

REPRESENTATIONS

Employee represents that Employee is not a party to any agreement,
restrictive covenant, or other obligation that would prevent or
restrict Employee from accepting employment with the Company or from
performing the duties described in this Agreement, and that Employee
has not brought, and will not use in connection with Employee's work
for the Company, any confidential or proprietary information belonging
to any prior employer. Employee further represents that Employee
has disclosed to the Company, prior to signing this Agreement, the
existence of any restrictive covenant to which Employee is currently
subject, and that none of those covenants conflicts with Employee's
employment by the Company in the position described above.

NON-COMPETITION

During Employee's employment with the Company and for a period of
eighteen (18) months following the termination of Employee's employment
for any reason (the Restricted Period), Employee shall not, directly
or indirectly, be employed by, consult for, own an interest in, or
otherwise provide services to any facility located anywhere in the
United States that designs or manufactures Competing Products, where
Employee's work at or for that facility would involve manufacturing
process engineering, tooling design, or a function substantially similar
to the function Employee performed for the Company. This restriction
applies regardless of the location of the entity that operates the
facility, regardless of the size of the facility, and applies to any
facility meeting the description above, wherever situated within the
United States. Employee acknowledges that the Company competes for
manufacturing programs on a nationwide basis, that Competing Products
are designed and manufactured at facilities throughout the United
States, and that a restriction limited to a smaller geographic area
would not adequately protect the Company's proprietary manufacturing
processes and confidential program information.

NONDISCLOSURE OF COMPANY INFORMATION

Nondisclosure of Company Information. During employment and at all
times thereafter, Employee shall not use, disclose, publish, or make
accessible to any third party any Proprietary Information, except to
the extent necessary to perform Employee's assigned duties for the
Company or as specifically authorized in writing by an officer of the
Company. Proprietary Information includes, without limitation: (i)
technical information such as product formulations, engineering data,
designs, prototypes, and research results; (ii) business information
such as customer and prospect identities and requirements, pricing,
margins, supplier terms, and strategic or financial plans; and
(iii) any other nonpublic information that the Company treats as
confidential. Employee's obligations under this Section do not apply to
information that (a) is or becomes generally known to the public through
no fault of Employee, (b) was lawfully in Employee's possession before
disclosure by the Company, free of any obligation of confidence, or (c)
is required to be disclosed by valid legal process, provided Employee
gives the Company prompt notice and a reasonable opportunity to seek
a protective order before disclosure occurs. Nothing in this Section
limits any protection afforded to trade secrets under applicable law,
and as to trade secrets this Section's restrictions continue for as
long as the information remains a trade secret.

ASSIGNMENT OF INVENTIONS

Assignment of Inventions. Employee agrees that all inventions,
discoveries, designs, developments, improvements, and works of
authorship, whether or not patentable or copyrightable, that Employee
conceives, develops, or reduces to practice, alone or with others,
during the period of Employee's employment with the Company and that
relate to the Company's actual or anticipated business, or that result
from any work performed by Employee for the Company, shall be the sole
and exclusive property of the Company. Employee hereby assigns to the
Company all right, title, and interest in and to any such inventions,
and agrees to execute any documents and take any actions reasonably
requested by the Company to perfect the Company's ownership rights
therein, both during and after employment.

TOLLING DURING BREACH

Tolling. If Employee breaches any restrictive covenant contained in this
Agreement, the period of restriction applicable to that covenant shall
be extended, or tolled, by the length of time during which Employee
was in breach, so that the Company receives the full benefit of the
bargained-for restriction. This tolling shall apply automatically
and without the need for further action by the Company, and shall
run from the date a court of competent jurisdiction determines that
a breach occurred, or from the date the breach ceases, whichever the
Company elects.

INJUNCTIVE RELIEF

Injunctive Relief. Employee acknowledges that a breach or threatened
breach of the restrictive covenants in this Agreement would cause
the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would not be
an adequate remedy. Accordingly, in the event of any such breach
or threatened breach, the Company shall be entitled, in addition
to any other rights and remedies available at law or in equity,
to seek temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive relief
restraining Employee from committing or continuing any such breach,
without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without
the necessity of proving actual damages. Nothing in this Section shall
be construed to limit the Company's right to pursue any other remedy
available to it, including the recovery of damages.

FORUM SELECTION

Forum Selection. Each party consents to the exclusive jurisdiction of
the state and federal courts located in the state whose law governs
this Agreement under the Governing Law Section above, for any action
arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection
to venue or personal jurisdiction in those courts.

SEVERABILITY

Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held by a court
of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable,
that provision shall be struck from this Agreement, and the remaining
provisions shall continue in full force and effect according to
their terms.

GOVERNING LAW

Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in
accordance with, the laws of the State of Connecticut, without giving
effect to any choice-of-law or conflict-of-laws provision that would
require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Employee acknowledges that Employee has read this Agreement, understands
its terms, has had the opportunity to consult with an attorney of
Employee's own choosing before signing it, and is entering into it
voluntarily in exchange for the consideration described above. Employee
acknowledges that Employee's duties as a Manufacturing Process Engineer
will give Employee access to manufacturing process information and
program information that the Company treats as confidential and that
would be valuable to a competing facility anywhere in the United
States, and that the restrictions in this Agreement are intended to
protect that information. Employee further acknowledges that Employee
received this Agreement and had a reasonable period of time to review
it before Employee's first day of employment with the Company.
