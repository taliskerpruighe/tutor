# How to read this

*v0.1.0*

Everything here is driven from the keyboard, and there are about nine keys
worth knowing. This page is the whole of it.

## Getting around

Across the top are the two **levels** of the course. Down the left are the
**parts** of the level you are on — and under whichever part you are in, the
**sections** and the **articles** inside it. This panel is the article.

Only the part you are reading opens up. The others stay as single lines, so
the whole level is in front of you without any of it being in the way.

Read it like a book if you like: press `n` for the next article and keep
pressing it. It will carry you through every part in order and stop at the
end.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `n` `p` | next / previous article |
| `m` | mark this article as read, press again to clear |
| `←` `→` | previous / next level |
| `[` `]` | previous / next part |
| `⇥` | next section |
| `1`–`9` | jump to that number in the section you are in |
| `↑` `↓` | scroll this panel |
| `/` | search everything |
| `?` | the full list of keys |
| `q` | quit |

The mouse works too. Click a level along the top, click a part, a section or
an article down the left, and the wheel scrolls this panel.

Nothing here can break anything. Press keys and find out what they do.

## Keeping track of what you have read

Nothing here is marked for you. Press `m` on an article once you have
finished it, and a tick (`✓`) appears next to its number in the list on the
left. Press `m` again to take the tick off, if you change your mind.

The ticks are still there next time you open this wiki. Closing it,
reinstalling it, or updating to a later version does not clear them.

## What the formatting means

Anything in `this typeface` is something you type or something the computer
said. Whole blocks of it are commands you can copy:

```
cd ~/tutor
tutor doctor
```

> Indented blocks like this one are asides — useful, but you can skip them on
> a first pass and come back later.

Occasionally there will be a link, like the [Claude Code
docs](https://docs.claude.com/claude-code). The grey text after it is the
address, so you can type it into a browser.

## Coming back to this later

Press `q` whenever you want to stop. To open it again, start **Ghostty** —
your terminal — and type:

```
cd ~/tutor && tutor
```

Press Enter. It opens on the first article, the same as it always does —
this wiki does not remember where you were. What it does remember is which
articles you have marked read: press `m` on the ones you finish, and their
ticks will still be there.

## Where to start

- If the terminal itself is unfamiliar, press `n` and keep going. The
  next part, **TUIs**, starts at the window you are reading this in.
- If you are comfortable there already, press `]` until you reach
  **Claude**, which is where Claude Code proper begins.
- If you are curious what operating system is actually running all
  this, the **Linux** part covers it.
- Everything in **Level 2**, across the top, assumes Level 1. Come back
  to it.

Reading is not the only way in.

Press `n`.
